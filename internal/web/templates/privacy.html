Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Soundcheck is a small personal project that emails you when artists you follow announce shows near you. This page explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send you concert alerts.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure hash. We can never see the password itself.
  • Your city and the artists you follow, to find shows for you.
  • Your name and email from Google, if you sign in with Google. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Who else is involved

  • Ticketmaster provides concert data. We only send them artist names and cities, never your personal details.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Railway hosts the app and the database.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose it.

We never sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account anytime in Settings. That removes your email, city and artist list from our database right away.
